Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our personal automation application handles information when you use our services or authenticate via Google OAuth.

1. Information We Access

Our application interfaces with third-party APIs (such as the YouTube Data API) solely to execute automated publishing scripts and manage authorized tokens. We only access data explicitly permitted by the scopes you authorize during the authentication flow.

2. Data Storage and Security

All authentication tokens (including OAuth refresh and access tokens) are stored locally in encrypted or secure runtime environments on private hardware. We do not transmit, sell, rent, or share your personal data or account tokens with any third parties.

3. Data Deletion

You can revoke access to your data at any time by visiting your Google Account Permissions page. Deleting local application token files instantly terminates local session access.

4. Contact

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, you may review our source repository or administration setup.
